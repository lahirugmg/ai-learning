# Acme Robotics: Time Off

New hires receive 23 vacation days per year, increasing to 28 days after five years of service.

Unused vacation days roll over until March 31 of the following year, with a limit of 7 days.

Sick leave is not capped, but a doctor's note is required for absences longer than 4 consecutive working days.

Every employee gets the second Friday of each month off, known internally as Maintenance Friday.
