# Acme Robotics: Expenses

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $62 per day, and receipts are required for any single purchase above $15.

Expense reports must be submitted through the Ledgerly app within 21 days of the purchase. Late reports need approval from a director.

Flights longer than 7 hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class is not reimbursed.

Employees get a one-time home office allowance of $475, which can be claimed after the first 90 days.
